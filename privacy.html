Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026.

Nitrate Online ("we", "us", "the site") is an archive of film criticism published between 1996 and 2004, available at nitrateonline.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to understand how the archive is used and to keep it online.

Information We Collect

The site does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or payment information through the site. Information is collected automatically in two ways:

  • Server and security logs. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, the pages you request, your browser type, and timestamps in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics and measurement. We use third-party analytics tools (described below) that set cookies and collect usage data to help us understand which reviews and features are read.

Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We do not use cookies for advertising or to sell your data. The site uses the following non-essential analytics services, which set their own cookies and process data on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland/LLC) — aggregate traffic measurement: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referral source. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) — usage analytics and aggregated session replay/heatmaps that show how visitors navigate pages, so we can improve the layout. Clarity masks text input by default. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

These are "non-essential" cookies: the archive works without them. They are used only to measure and improve the site.

Your Choices

You can limit or refuse non-essential cookies in several ways:

  • Browser controls. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. Refer to your browser's settings.
  • Google Analytics opt-out. Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent Google Analytics from collecting data across sites.
  • Do not track / privacy extensions. Browser privacy extensions and tracker-blocking features will generally block both Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.

Blocking these services does not affect your ability to read anything on the site.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data — including the right to access, correct, or delete it, to object to or restrict its processing, and (for California and similar U.S. state residents) the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Data Retention and Transfers

Analytics data is retained by our providers according to their own policies and the retention settings we configure. Because Google, Microsoft, and Cloudflare are global companies, your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards those providers maintain.

Children's Privacy

The site is a general-audience archive and is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to Other Sites

The archive links to external websites (for example, other film resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; this policy applies only to Nitrate Online.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact nitrate@nitrateonline.com.
